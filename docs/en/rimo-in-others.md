# Rimo in Others

[English](../en/rimo-in-others.md) | [日本語](../ja/rimo-in-others.md)

Rimo works in more places than the ones with their own page in the sidebar. This page collects the rest — pick the tool you use and follow its guide.

| Tool | What it gives you | Guide |
|------|-------------------|-------|
| **Genspark** | Ask questions about your meetings and your past notes from Super Agent. You add Rimo yourself as a custom MCP server under **Community & Custom**. | [Genspark](rimo-in-genspark.md) |
| **Notion** | Ask questions about your meetings and find information from your past notes. Needs a workspace on a plan that includes MCP connections. | [Notion](rimo-in-notion.md) |
| **Slack** | Ask questions about your meetings, decisions, action items, and past discussions. Whether Rimo is reachable depends on your workspace setup — the guide covers what to check. | [Slack](rimo-in-slack.md) |

> **This list grows.** Each tool gets its own page as it's verified end to end, and shows up here. If the tool you use isn't listed yet, the [hosted MCP server](mcp.md) works with any client that speaks remote MCP — and [Rimo in Microsoft Copilot](rimo-in-microsoft.md) is the closest example of wiring one up by hand.
